Our Journey

Founded in 1999, National Dial has grown from a small manufacturing unit to a leading supplier of precision dials in India. Over the years, we have expanded our product range, upgraded our technology, and built a reputation for quality and reliability. Today, we serve clients across pharmaceutical, industrial, food, and automotive sectors, delivering both standard and custom dial solutions.

  • 1999: Company founded in Pune, Maharashtra
  • 2005: Expanded to new manufacturing facility
  • 2010: Launched custom dial solutions
  • 2020: Surpassed 1000+ clients and 500+ completed projects
