Case Study: Improving Safety & Efficiency for an Industrial Client

Industry: Industrial Manufacturing

Client Challenge: The client needed highly durable and clear gauge glass for monitoring liquid levels in high-pressure boilers. Their previous glass often failed, causing downtime and safety risks.

Our Solution: National Glass supplied custom Reflex Gauge Glass made from Borosilicate 3.3, engineered for high pressure and temperature. The glass was tailored to fit their assemblies and delivered with full compliance to industry standards.

Results:

  • Zero failures in 2 years of operation
  • Improved operator safety
  • Reduced maintenance costs
  • Full compliance with safety regulations
