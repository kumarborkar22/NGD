About Us

Over 25 Years of Excellence

National Glass is a leading manufacturer specializing in high-quality Reflex Gauge Glass, Transparent Gauge Glass, and Round Sight Glass. With over 25 years of industry experience, we are committed to delivering precision-engineered products that meet the highest standards of durability, safety, and performance. Our expertise, combined with advanced manufacturing processes, ensures reliable solutions trusted by clients across industrial, pharmaceutical, food, and automobile sectors.

  • Reflex Gauge Glass (Toughened, Soda Lime, Borosilicate 3.3/4.2)
  • Transparent Gauge Glass
  • Round Sight Glass (2mm to 25mm, Borosilicate & Soda Lime)
  • Step Glass
  • Oil Level Sight Glass
  • Customized glass as per customer drawing

Our Journey

Founded in 1999, National Glass has grown from a small manufacturing unit to a leading supplier of Reflex Gauge Glass, Transparent Gauge Glass, and Round Sight Glass in India. Over the years, we have expanded our product range, upgraded our technology, and built a reputation for quality and reliability. Today, we serve clients across pharmaceutical, industrial, food, and automobile sectors, delivering both standard and custom glass solutions.

  • 1995: Company founded in Pune, Maharashtra
  • 2005: Expanded to new manufacturing facility
  • 2010: Launched custom glass solutions
  • 2020: Surpassed 1000+ clients and 500+ completed projects
