Reflex Gauge Glass

Reflex gauge glass is engineered for precise and reliable liquid level indication in industrial equipment. Its unique design uses the principle of light refraction to clearly distinguish between liquid and vapor phases. Toughened for durability, it withstands high pressure and temperature, making it ideal for boilers, tanks, and pressure vessels. Easy installation and long service life make it a preferred choice for demanding applications.

  • Toughened, available in Soda Lime and Borosilicate (3.3/4.2)
  • For clear and accurate liquid level indication
  • Works on light refraction principle for clear visibility
  • Suitable for boilers, tanks, and pressure vessels
  • High resistance to pressure and temperature
  • Durable and long-lasting performance
  • Ideal for steam and liquid applications
  • Easy installation and maintenance

Transparent Gauge Glass

Transparent gauge glass offers high clarity for monitoring liquid levels, providing a clear view of both liquid and vapor. Manufactured from premium Borosilicate or Soda Lime, it is resistant to thermal shock and mechanical stress. This glass is commonly used in boilers, tanks, and industrial vessels where accurate level indication is critical. Its robust construction ensures long-lasting performance in harsh environments.

  • High-clarity glass for liquid level indication
  • Available in Borosilicate and Soda Lime
  • Provides clear distinction between liquid and vapor phases
  • Commonly used in boilers, tanks, and industrial vessels
  • Made from high-quality, durable glass for long service life
  • Resistant to thermal shock and mechanical stress

Round Sight Glass

Round sight glass enables visual inspection of fluid flow in pipelines, reactors, and process equipment. Available in a wide range of sizes and materials, it is designed for high transparency and durability. Its resistance to pressure, temperature, and chemicals makes it suitable for pharmaceutical, food, and industrial applications. Precision manufacturing ensures reliable performance and safety.

  • Size range: 2mm to 25mm
  • Available in Borosilicate and Soda Lime
  • Designed for pharmaceutical industry applications
  • Provides clear visual inspection of fluid flow
  • High transparency and excellent durability
  • Resistant to pressure, temperature, and chemicals
  • Suitable for pipelines, reactors, and process equipment
  • Manufactured with precision for reliable performance

Step Glass

Step glass is specially designed for liquid level indication in gauge assemblies, featuring a stepped surface that enhances visibility through light reflection. It provides a clear distinction between liquid and vapor, even under high-pressure and high-temperature conditions. Step glass is easy to install and replace, and its durable construction ensures a long service life in industrial settings.

  • Specially designed for liquid level indication in gauge assemblies
  • Stepped surface enhances visibility through light reflection
  • Provides clear distinction between liquid and vapor phases
  • Suitable for high-pressure and high-temperature applications
  • Commonly used in boilers, tanks, and industrial vessels
  • Made from high-quality, durable glass for long service life
  • Resistant to thermal shock and mechanical stress
  • Easy to install and replace

Oil Level Sight Glass

Oil level sight glass is used for monitoring oil levels in industrial machinery and tanks. It provides a clear and reliable indication of oil presence, helping to ensure safe and efficient operation. Built to withstand harsh environments, it is easy to install and maintain. Its robust design makes it suitable for a wide range of industrial applications.

  • For monitoring oil levels in industrial equipment
  • Provides clear and reliable oil level indication
  • Durable construction for harsh environments
  • Easy to install and maintain
