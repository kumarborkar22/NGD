What we do

At National Glass, we specialize in the manufacturing of high-quality Reflex Gauge Glass, Transparent Gauge Glass, Round Sight Glass, and Step Glass. Our products are engineered for durability, safety, and performance, serving industries such as industrial, pharmaceutical, food, and automobile sectors. With over 25 years of experience, we deliver customized glass solutions to meet the unique needs of our clients.

Reflex Gauge Glass

Toughened, available in Soda Lime and Borosilicate (3.3/4.2). For clear and accurate liquid level indication in boilers, tanks, and pressure vessels.

Transparent Gauge Glass

High-clarity glass for liquid level indication, available in Borosilicate and Soda Lime.

Round Sight Glass

Size range: 2mm to 25mm, available in Borosilicate and Soda Lime. Used for visual inspection in pipelines, reactors, and process equipment.

Step Glass

Specially designed for gauge assemblies, enhances visibility of liquid/vapor phases.

Oil Level Sight Glass

For monitoring oil levels in industrial equipment.
